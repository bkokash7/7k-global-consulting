About 7K Global Consulting

7K Global Consulting provides independent advisory services across four practices: construction oversight, intercultural consulting, strategic research, and applied AI systems.

This range comes from experience. Over 20 years managing facilities and construction projects internationally, combined with direct cross-cultural work and a background in technical systems development, shaped these four practices.

That experience goes into every client relationship: practical guidance, grounded in real work, applied to the project in front of us.

7K Global Consulting operates as a DBA of Seven Kilo Enterprises LLC.

إعداد تشغيل وصيانة المبنى

مبنى يُسلَّم بدون إطار تشغيل هو مسؤولية في انتظار الوقوع. نساعد في إنشاء أنظمة إدارة المنشآت والصيانة منذ اليوم الأول.

طلب استشارة

توثيق FM

  • Asset register and equipment inventory
  • MEP system diagrams and shutoff locations
  • Contractor and vendor contact log
  • Warranty register with expiry tracking

الصيانة الوقائية

  • HVAC filter and service intervals
  • Plumbing inspection and flushing schedules
  • Electrical panel and earthing checks
  • Fire system testing and inspection calendar
  • Roof, façade, and drain inspection schedule

أنظمة التنظيف

  • Scope of work by area type and frequency
  • Cleaning product specification by surface
  • Equipment list and storage requirements
  • Quality check and inspection protocol

الاستجابة للطوارئ

  • Power failure and generator protocol
  • Water leak and shutoff response procedure
  • Fire alarm and evacuation coordination
  • Security breach response outline

تدريب المشغلين

  • Building operator walkthrough training
  • System operation guides per equipment
  • Tenant/resident onboarding documentation
  • Escalation and reporting structure

أنواع المباني

  • Commercial office and retail buildings
  • Residential apartment complexes
  • Boutique hospitality properties
  • Mixed-use developments
  • High-value standalone residential

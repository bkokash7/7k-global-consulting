Developer Advisory

Independent completion oversight for developers. We reduce preventable rework, strengthen subcontractor accountability, and improve handover quality, which protects both your margins and your reputation.

Why Developers Use Independent Oversight

Margin Protection

Rework costs 5–20% of contract value on unaudited builds. Catching issues before they compound protects profit margins directly.

Warranty Risk Reduction

Post-handover callbacks, defect liability claims, and tenant complaints are predictable, and largely preventable. Independent verification before turnover closes the exposure.

Buyer & Tenant Confidence

A structured, documented handover strengthens delivery perception and brand reputation. In competitive markets, buyers notice which developer hands over a clean unit.

Engagement Formats
Per-unit completion audit (Tier II through Final Tier)
Multi-unit program with standardized reporting across the development
Retained completion advisory during active delivery windows
Select stage-gate add-ons at critical construction milestones
Full Completion Integrity Program™ on flagship or high-value units

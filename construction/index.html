The Case for Independent Oversight

Most of it is invisible
at handover.

In fast-developing markets, timelines are compressed, subcontractor chains are long, and finishing quality suffers under schedule pressure. Undersized ducts, failed waterproofing, inadequate fire barriers, improper grounding: none of it shows on handover day, and all of it is expensive once the walls are closed.

7K Global Consulting provides the independent verification layer that protects owners, investors, buyers, and developers from the consequences of what nobody checked.

Catch It Before It's Closed

Concealed systems can be verified before walls close. After closure, correcting them means demolition, at several times the cost of prevention.

Documentation That Protects You

Our reports create a traceable record. In disputes, warranty claims, or resale, documentation is the difference between a position and a winnable case.

Independent, Not Conflicted

We work for you, not the contractor. No financial interest in overlooking deficiencies. No incentive to accept what shouldn't be accepted.

Advisory Costs Less Than Rework

On a $2M project, rework commonly runs $100K to $400K. Our advisory fee is a small fraction of that exposure.

Stage-Gate Inspections

Selective milestone verification at critical construction stages. Engage them as add-ons to reduce risk before completion, without committing to a full-tier program.

Schedule Consultation Back to Construction Hub
Typical Milestone Reviews
Foundation and early structural milestones, with observational verification and documentation
Waterproofing verification prior to slab or wall closure
MEP rough-in reviews (electrical, plumbing, HVAC) prior to finishing
Thermal insulation and building envelope before external closure
Pre-finishing verification before the final completion push
Pre-handover inspection and turnover readiness checks
Stage-gate inspections are structured as advisory verification and documentation reporting. Final responsibilities remain with licensed professionals and executing contractors.
